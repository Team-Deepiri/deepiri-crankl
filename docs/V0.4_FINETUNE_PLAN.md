## The blocker (why finetune is fake today)

Pack and holonomy disagree on what a crank *means*:

- `src/pack/fold.cpp` optimizes multivector fields vs 8 floats/slot
- `src/pack/unfold.cpp` inverts that (not decrank)
- `src/holonomy/wilson.cpp` uses `decrank_matrix` — the actual GUCT operator

**Until pack minimizes `||W - decrank(C)||²` per 8×8 tile, turn/holonomy finetune is theater.**

GUCT target objective:

> J(C) = ‖W − decrank(C)‖² + λ·W₂(PD) + μ·β₁

Turn update:

> C_new = C_old ⊗ exp(dL/dC) via BCH, dL/dC ∈ Lie algebra (bivector generators)

## Training answer
